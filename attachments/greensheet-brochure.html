Section 01 — Position

Sourcing green coffee has looked more like gambling than strategy.

Paper ledgers, forked spreadsheets, and offers that live and die inside a messaging thread. Roasters commit real capital against claims nobody can check.

Asserted

“Exceptional micro-lot. Jasmine, stone fruit, silky body. Limited availability.”

Verified
  • cup score 86.25 SCA
  • moisture 10.8%
  • screen 15/16
  • process washed, 36h ferment
  • safra 2026 · lot 340 × 69kg

The offer sheet has been the trade's working instrument for a century. We didn't replace it — we raised its standard of proof. Sensory detail, quality claims, and logistics become strict, comparable, checkable data points, held in one system of record.

That single change has a consequence the industry has been circling for years: once you can verify what is in a lot, you can also verify what went into it. The four positions on the next sheet are what Greensheet is built to hold accountable.

Section 02 — Positions, entered as line items

Four claims we're willing to be held to.

POS-01
Position

Value is created before it is tasted.

Farmers, workers, ecosystems, and institutions generate value along the entire chain — not the final palate at the end of it. A Robusta block in Vietnam with planned irrigation, disciplined pruning, and cared-for soil has already produced the conditions for specialty quality. That work holds value before a single bean is roasted or scored.

On the sheet

Lot records carry the productive history — practice, input, and stewardship at origin — not only the outcome in the cup.

POS-02
Position

Price is a signal, not a verdict.

The C-market and the specialty premium both routinely fail to describe what production actually cost or contributed. Treating price as the whole account is a habit, not a finding. It can be tested.

On the sheet

Support for self-verification, true-cost accounting, and social value accounting — so a number can be read against the work that produced it.

POS-03
Position

Coffee is a system, not a commodity.

Roads. Drying beds. Fermentation labs. Research stations. Cooperative structures. A cup is the visible tip of an iceberg whose mass is investment made by farmers, governments, and collectives — and almost none of it appears on a conventional offer.

On the sheet

Infrastructure and institutional context are part of the lot record, because they are part of the lot.

POS-04
Position

Trade should reinvest, not extract.

Conventional models move surplus away from the people who made it and toward roasters and brands. The useful question isn't what premium was paid — it's how much came back. Profit-sharing structures and origin reinvestment funds answer it in a way a premium never has to.

On the sheet

Reinvestment is a reportable field, not a press release. What returns to origin is recorded and auditable.

Section 03 — Method

From published lot to standing relationship.

Verification is the beginning, not the product. What follows is the part most sourcing tools leave to memory and goodwill: getting coffee into a roaster's hands, capturing what they actually tasted, and acting on it while it's still fresh.

  1. 01 · Publish

    The lot goes on record

    Importers and exporters publish lots with verified metrics instead of adjectives. Roasters browse, filter, and compare on like-for-like terms.

  2. 02 · Sample

    Sample kits ship on their own

    Qualified interest triggers fulfillment and routing automatically. No chasing, no spreadsheet of who received what.

  3. 03 · Cup

    Feedback arrives structured

    Guided evaluation four days after delivery, once the coffee has rested. What comes back is comparable data, not a thumbs-up in a text message.

  4. 04 · Match

    The shortlist lands while the palate is warm

    Within two hours of feedback, the roaster sees lots matched to what they just described liking. Sensory recency is the whole advantage.

  5. 05 · Return

    Reorder rhythm and peer referral

    Reorder timing follows actual consumption. Roasters who are having a good year introduce the ones who should be.

The same ledger, pointed at origin

Greensheet keeps a tamper-evident, hash-chained log of every consequential action — price changes, data revisions, consent events — exported nightly to immutable storage. That machinery exists for audit readiness. It works just as well as proof that a reinvestment commitment was honored, which is the difference between a value and a claim about one.
