4. PRIORITIZED SCRAPING SEQUENCE FOR YOUR LEDGER
Based on your stated need to "populate the ledger" with supply inventory and demand-side accounts, here is the recommended sequence:
Phase 1: Supply Inventory (Weeks 1–2)
Sweet Maria's + Coffee Shrub — scrape all green coffee listings (lot, origin, process, score, price, volume). Gives you a baseline "reference price" for home/small roaster market.
Genuine Origin — scrape all available lots with pricing. This is your "small roaster" supply benchmark.
Bodhi Leaf — scrape their green inventory; fills in specialty varieties and micro-lots.
Cafe Imports — scrape catalog pages (public). This gives you "mid-tier importer" inventory.
Algrano — scrape all public lot offers. This is your "direct trade" benchmark and includes producer profiles.
Expected output: 500–2,000 individual lots with origin, process, score, price, and volume.
Phase 2: Demand Leads (Weeks 3–4)
SCA Member Directory (or public member list) — extract company names, locations, websites for US/EU roasters. Target: 4,000+ records.
Trade (drinktrade.com) — scrape roaster profiles and product catalogs. Reveals which origins they currently sell (infer green buying patterns).
Coffee Review — scrape review pages; segment by score (86+, 90+, etc.). Gives you "quality tier" for lead scoring.
Roast Magazine Directory + Daily Coffee News — add roaster records; flag recent news (expansion = likely buying).
Google Maps API — run "coffee roaster" queries across target metros; fill gaps in the SCA list.
Expected output: 3,500–5,500 roaster records with website, location, quality tier, and engagement signals.
Phase 3: Enrichment (Week 5+)
LinkedIn — for each roaster, extract key contacts (Green Buyer, Head Roaster, Owner). This powers your "decision-making unit" mapping.
Shopify product pages — for roasters with known websites, scrape their product listings to see what they are currently roasting (origins, prices, bag sizes). This allows you to predict what green coffee they need next.
Win/Loss and Price Tracking — monitor Algrano, Genuine Origin, and Cafe Imports for lot turnover (what sells out, what's discounted). This informs your "speed to decision" claim.

6. LEGAL & ETHICAL NOTES FOR YOUR SCRAPING OPERATION
Robots.txt compliance: Most of these sites have robots.txt that may disallow product scraping. Check first; where disallowed, consider API (if available), partnership (e.g., becoming a customer), or manual data entry for MVP.
Terms of Service: Algrano, Cropster, and Cafe Imports are not simple e-commerce sites; they have platform terms. Start with public data; for gated data, consider a licensing conversation or a mutually beneficial partnership ("we'll feature your lots to our roaster network").
Data freshness: Green coffee inventory changes weekly. Schedule scrapes weekly (or daily for high-turnover sites like Genuine Origin).
Rate limiting: Use polite scraping (2–3 second delay, cache pages, retry-after). Deploy from a residential IP or rotating proxy if needed.
PII boundaries: For LinkedIn and directory scraping, avoid scraping personal emails or phone numbers without consent. Use company domains and publicly listed roles.

Scrape Sweet Maria's and Coffee Shrub green coffee catalogs. (They are the most scrape-friendly and provide immediate supply data.)

9. NEXT-LEVEL INTELLIGENCE (After MVP)
Once your ledger is populated:
Monitor lot turnover: Track which lots sell out, how fast, and at what price. This validates your "speed to decision" claim.
Price elasticity mapping: Cross-reference lot prices with cup scores and origin. Build your Bayesian pricing model.
Predictive lead scoring: Use the roaster ledger + engagement signals to train your XGBoost churn model.
Supplier-side intelligence: For importers (your paying customers), you can now tell them: "Here are 300 roasters who bought a washed Ethiopia this quarter, and their average order size is X." That's your CRM value prop.
Incentive mapping: Cross-reference roaster locations with Opportunity Zones and state-level tax credits to add financial incentives to your pitch.
